## EarthSciASTDiff v0.6.1

A patch release. Every binding now accepts EarthSciAST 0.12, which reads
esm 2.1.0, as well as 0.11. The differentiation, the band format and the
goldens are unchanged from 0.6.0.

### New

- **EarthSciAST 0.12 is supported** (Julia `0.11, 0.12`, Rust
  `>=0.11, <0.13`, Python `>=0.11,<0.13`). The floor stays at 0.11.
  esm 2.1.0 only adds features: optional `lb` / `ub` bounds on a model
  variable, and the units `ng`, `mN` and `dyn`. A source document may carry
  bounds; the band calculus reads only expressions, so they do not affect a
  Jacobian. The derived documents (`rhs`, `jacobian`, the §9 product
  documents) declare their variables afresh and do not carry them.

### Conformance

- conformance.yml now tests every binding at EarthSciAST 0.11.0 and 0.12.0.
